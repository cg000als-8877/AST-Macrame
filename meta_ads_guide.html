2. Step-by-Step Meta Ads Manager Setup

1
Open Meta Ads Manager & Create Campaign

1. Go to adsmanager.facebook.com on your computer.

2. Click the green + Create button.

3. Choose Sales as the campaign objective (Do NOT choose Traffic or Engagement, as Sales gives real buying customers).

4. Choose Manual Sales campaign and click Continue.

5. Name your campaign: AST Macrame - Video Retail Campaign - [Month].

2
Ad Set Level Setup (Pixel & Audience)

A. Conversion Settings:

  • Conversion Location: Select Website.
  • Pixel: Select your AST Macrame Pixel.
  • Conversion Event: Select Purchase.
💡 Pro Tip on Pixel: Since we already integrated the Pixel into your code, the Purchase event automatically triggers when a customer submits an order on your Retail page!

B. Budget & Schedule:

  • Set Daily Budget to $5.00 to $10.00 (or ৳ 600 to ৳ 1,200).
  • Set Start Time to 12:01 AM (Midnight) so Meta spends budget evenly throughout the day.

C. Audience Targeting:

  • Location: Bangladesh (Include all).
  • Age: 20 – 45.
  • Detailed Targeting (Interests): Keep it Broad OR test keywords: Online shopping, Fashion accessories, Denim, Casual wear.
3
Ad Level (Connecting Your Posted Video & Order Now Button)

This is where you connect your published Facebook video post with the website link:

1. Identity: Select your official Facebook Page and connected Instagram Account.

2. Ad Setup:

  • Option A (Recommended for Social Proof): Select "Use Existing Post" → Click "Select Post" → Choose your uploaded Video post.
  • Option B: Select "Create Ad" → Upload your video file fresh.

3. Call to Action Button:

  • Select "Order Now" or "Shop Now".

4. Website URL:

Enter your website landing page URL. Add tracking UTM tags to track sales sources accurately:

https://yourwebsite.com/retail?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=video_ad&utm_campaign=macrame_belt

5. Tracking: Ensure Website Events (Meta Pixel) is checked and active with a green indicator.

6. Click Publish!

5. Post-Launch Optimization Rules

Rule 1: Don't Touch the Ad for First 48 Hours
Meta's AI needs 48 hours to learn and find the cheapest buyers. Don't pause or edit budget during this learning phase.

Rule 2: How to Scale Budget
If your Cost Per Purchase is below ৳ 150 - ৳ 200 and you are getting steady orders, increase the daily budget by 20% every 3 days (never double it instantly as it resets learning).
